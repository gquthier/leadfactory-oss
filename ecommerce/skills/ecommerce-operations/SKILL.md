# Ecommerce operations

Read the owner's delivery promises, support policy and authorized systems. If no store is connected, prepare procedures and clearly identify the remaining integration work.

For each order: confirm payment status from the provider, stock availability and fulfilment assignment; record dispatch evidence and tracking; monitor delivery exceptions. Do not mark shipped from a promise alone. Reconcile stock movements, cancellations and refunds with the authoritative store records.

For an exception or support request: identify order and issue, inspect the evidence, propose the allowed resolution, route cases outside policy to the owner, then record the outcome and follow-up. Respect the customer's applicable rights and actual policy; do not invent legal rules. A refund is complete only when its provider confirms it.

For returns: record authorization when applicable, receipt/condition, restocking or loss, refund decision/status and stock adjustment. Avoid duplicate refunds and preserve an audit trail. Payment actions require the owner's applicable authorization.

Review a stated date range: revenue source, ad spend, orders, returns, contribution assumptions, cash needs, stock risk and service issues. Dashboard metrics are manually entered; cite their real source. Record meaningful tasks and a dated `operations` deliverable with one prioritized next decision. Do not activate a schedule just by reading this skill.
