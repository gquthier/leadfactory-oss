# Fictional worked handoff

Example only: Atelier Demo, a storage pouch for the French market. No sales, supplier or advertising observation is asserted.

## Research → decision

Create a candidate product with name, audience hypothesis and problem hypothesis. Research comparable products with the owner's available browser. Record each actual source and its date in competitor records. If no research tool is available, leave evidence unknown and create the research task.

Illustrative assumptions: net sale €29, landed product €7, shipping subsidy €4, payment fees €1 and expected return cost €2. Contribution before acquisition would be €15. A target acquisition cost of €10 would leave €5 contribution per order before fixed costs. These are fictional inputs, not a pricing recommendation or evidence of demand. Replace each with a source before a real decision.

The Director receives the evidence table, supplier questions, scenarios and a proposed bounded experiment. The product stays `researching` while critical facts are unknown.

## Decision → store preview

After the owner's product decision, Store Builder reads `shopify-setup`, then the relevant theme/catalog skills. Confirm the authorized store and CLI connection, create a separate development theme, implement a product page with verified copy, and populate draft products/variants. Record the actual theme identifier, files and preview URL in a store-stage deliverable.

Check product selection, variant price/availability, add/remove/cart quantity, shipping/tax disclosure, checkout in test mode, confirmation email and measurement event. Capture failures and fixes. A preview without a completed check remains `building` or `preview`; it is never labeled `live` merely because code exists.

## Preview → creative and launch handoff

Creative receives the approved product facts, objection map, asset rights and offer. Produce files using the owner's available media tools, inspect each render and link the actual files. A prompt-only output stays a brief, not a ready image/video.

Acquisition receives the preview QA evidence, creative files, measurement plan, target contribution and explicit budget limits. Prepare the campaign and its stop/review conditions. Publication or spending occurs only within the owner's authorization and with a successful provider response.

## Operations

Operations checks actual orders, stock, shipping exceptions and service requests in connected systems. Store a sourced dated review in `deliverables` and its next actions in `tasks`. Metrics entered in the dashboard remain marked as manually recorded, even when an agent transcribes them from an authorized report.

At each handoff, call the real runtime tools, then verify the returned record IDs. Give the next role the product ID, stage, deliverable ID, evidence, outstanding questions and acceptance condition.
