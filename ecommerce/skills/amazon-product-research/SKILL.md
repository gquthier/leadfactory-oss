# Product research from observed evidence

Adapted from Nexscope AI's MIT skill; see LICENSE.txt. Amazon is one evidence source, not a sales forecast for a different store.

1. Read the owner's market, currency, audience, budget and constraints. Reuse answers already provided.
2. With the available browser or connected research tool, inspect a bounded sample of comparable listings. Record URL, observation date, price, offer, rating, review count and availability. Read contrasting reviews to identify problems, objections and product limitations.
3. Keep observed facts separate from estimates. BSR, active ads, review counts and traffic estimates do not establish profit or actual sales. Label seasonality and cross-market assumptions.
4. Compare differentiation, supplier feasibility, acquisition assumptions and full contribution economics with `product-economics`. An unknown input remains an open task.
5. Read `commerce_schema`, create/update each candidate with `commerce_records`, and attach the source-based analysis as a deliverable. Give a conditional decision and the next smallest useful test.

If browsing or a paid dataset is unavailable, prepare the research table and name the missing inputs. Never manufacture evidence. This pack contains no scraper installation or paid research account.
