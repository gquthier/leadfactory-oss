# Reply qualification

Owner: Acquisition, with Delivery for the client-facing update. Use `outbound-reply-qualification` on actual messages linked to the right client and campaign.

Classify positive interest, question, objection, out-of-office, refusal/opt-out, bounce or uncertain reply. Record evidence, date, provider message ID and next action. Stop the affected sequence after a human reply; record an opt-out in the authorized suppression scope. Verify the provider receipt, or leave the operation explicitly pending if the tool is absent.

An opportunity handoff includes the expressed need, fit, unknowns and proposed conversation. A meeting is confirmed only with an actual reply/calendar receipt. A suggested slot or positive reply is not a sale. Keep internal qualifications separate from the outgoing draft.

Store detailed replies in the client's private folder; expose only the necessary summary in the cockpit task/deliverable and portfolio report. Do not read another client's contacts, reuse their private evidence or send outside the existing mandate.
